# Case study: small local AI models and conversational robotics

## Challenge
An interactive robotic companion requires more than a model producing text.
Audio capture, voice detection, transcription, inference, TTS, playback,
turn-taking and safe control all influence perceived responsiveness.

## Approach
Developed isolated adapters, feedback-safe audio experiments, model
inference workflows, expressive-intent interfaces and staged testing
under constrained device resources. Runtime QA distinguished individual
software PASS states from actual human and sensor acceptance.

## Outcome
Component integrations and controlled end-to-end experiments provided useful
technical evidence. Prompt-only personality work did not establish reliable
generalization under varied inputs. Full-duplex spoken barge-in and
complete subjective interaction acceptance remained open at closeout.

The engagement concluded and the engineering learnings were documented.
No claim of production certification or completed companion behavior.
